Meet the Team

The people behind every card — passionate collectors who know their stuff.

Nick Williams

Founder & Owner

Sake Kitty Cards is built for collectors — honest on condition, fair on price, and genuinely excited whenever someone finds a card they've been chasing. Nick built the brand on the values he grew up collecting with: people over transactions, care over speed, hands on every card.
