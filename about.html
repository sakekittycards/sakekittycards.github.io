Our Story

Sake Kitty started as one collector chasing one card. It grew into a crew of vendors with the same belief: the Pokémon market should feel honest and welcoming, not intimidating.

Every card is personally inspected, conservatively described, and packed to make the trip safely. Whether you're hunting an alt art, building a budget deck, or just cracking packs — you get the same care.
