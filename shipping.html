How to Pack Your Cards

Packed Right = Safe Delivery + Happy Customers

Proper packing protects your cards and helps us process your order quickly.

1 Card

Each card in its own top loader.

  1. Place the card in a clear plastic penny sleeve.
  2. Insert the sleeved card into a top loader.
  3. Place the top loader inside a team bag and seal with tape.
  4. Place the team-bagged card in a bubble mailer and seal.
Tip The team bag keeps the top loader clean and protects the card during transit.

Up to 10 Cards

Up to 10 cards in one team bag.

  1. Place each card in its own clear plastic sleeve.
  2. Place up to 10 sleeved cards in a team bag and seal with tape.
  3. Place the team-bagged cards in a bubble mailer and seal.
Tip Keep it to 10 or fewer per bag so cards stay flat and protected.

10 – 19 Cards

10 to 19 cards ship in a bubble mailer.

  1. Place each card in its own clear plastic sleeve.
  2. Place 10 to 19 sleeved cards in one or two team bags (10 max per bag) and seal with tape.
  3. Place the team-bagged cards in a bubble mailer and seal.
Tip Always seal the bubble mailer securely with tape.

20+ Cards / Large Collections

20+ cards up to 1,000+ cards.

  1. Sort and stack cards neatly in a card storage box, ETB, or similar sturdy container.
  2. Place the storage box inside a shipping box. Fill all empty space with packing paper or peanuts. No movement inside the box.
  3. Seal the box securely with tape. Double box valuable collections when possible.

    Bulk Packing Tips

  • Keep cards tight and secure.
  • No movement inside the box.
  • Use plenty of packing material.
  • Double box high-value collections.
  • Label the box clearly.

Do Not

  • Do not use Scotch tape on the top loader. It can damage the card and leave residue.
  • Do not put multiple cards in one top loader. Each card needs its own top loader or team bag.
  • Do not substitute Card Savers for top loaders on submissions.
  • Do not use ETB sleeves, Dragon Shield sleeves, or any other non-clear sleeves. Only use clear plastic penny sleeves.
  • Do not use rubber bands directly on cards.
  • Do not ship cards loose in an envelope.
  • Do not leave empty space in the box. Cards will shift and can get damaged.

Use Only Clear Sleeves

Use standard clear penny sleeves for all cards.

Team bags: resealable plastic bags used to protect top loaders.

No colored sleeves, no patterned sleeves, no ETB sleeves, no Dragon Shield — nothing but clear.

Shipping Tips

  • Use bubble mailers for small orders.
  • Use sturdy boxes for large collections.
  • Always get tracking and insurance for valuable shipments.

Extra Tips

  • Include your name / order # inside the package.
  • Double-check the address before shipping.
  • Take photos of packed items (helpful for claims).
  • Keep your tracking until delivery is confirmed.

Thank You!

Properly packed cards show you care about the hobby. We appreciate your business and trust.
