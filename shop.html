What You'll Find in Our Shop

Graded slabs — PSA, CGC, BGS & SGC

Authenticated, professionally graded Pokémon cards, from vintage Base Set holos to modern alt arts and special illustration rares. Each slab is listed by its real cert grade and shown in its own photo, so what you see is what ships — best for collectors who want condition guaranteed. New to grading? Try our grading prep service or read when it's worth grading a card.

Sealed product — boxes, ETBs & tins

Factory-sealed English and Japanese Pokémon: booster boxes, Elite Trainer Boxes, collection boxes, tins, and bundles. Great for rippers chasing fresh pulls and for collectors holding sealed long-term. Sealed orders ship double-boxed and fully insured — see how we pack & ship.

Raw singles

Ungraded single cards — vintage holos, trainer gallery pulls, full arts, and budget playset staples. Every single is hand-inspected and described conservatively (we grade condition down, never up). Want more? Hundreds of additional singles live on our TCGplayer storefront.

Apparel & merch

Original Sake Kitty designs — tees and accessories made to order. This is the shop's fixed-price, add-to-cart category; graded slabs and singles are typically make-an-offer so you can name your price.

Why buy from Sake Kitty Cards? We're collectors who run booths at Florida card shows and ship nationwide. Every order is hand-checked before it leaves, conditions are described honestly, and cards and sealed ship fully insured (free over $100). Questions first? Contact us or browse the FAQ. Looking to sell instead of buy? We buy and trade singles, sealed, graded, and bulk — cash or store credit.
